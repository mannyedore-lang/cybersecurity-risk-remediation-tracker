# Security Remediation Governance Cadence

## Weekly Working Session
- Review critical/high findings
- Confirm evidence status
- Resolve technical blockers
- Update due dates and owners

## Biweekly Leadership Review
- Highlight overdue high-risk items
- Review risk acceptance requests
- Escalate cross-functional dependencies

## Monthly Executive Review
- Risk trend
- Closure rate
- Aging findings
- Control domains at risk
- Decisions / funding needed
